Frozen question/configuration
RMS averages across the swatches: one equally inaccurate swatch contributes less to a four-color error than a two-color error. Compare the examples before calibrating. Rendering and grading share canonical 8-bit sRGB, so very small HSL changes can produce the same displayed color. No Firebase or audio is loaded, and these settings never change a match.
