How to play

  • Bold lines split the grid into rooms; each holds exactly one mirror ( or ).
  • A laser enters at a letter, reflects 90° off mirrors, and must leave at the same letter. A number is how many times that beam reflects.
  • Click a cell to cycle empty → → → → empty. The marks a cell you've ruled out. A room must hold exactly one mirror — a second is allowed briefly, but is flagged as a conflict if it lingers. Blue mirrors are given.
  • Beams may cross on empty cells. Solve when every laser reaches its pair with the right reflection count.
