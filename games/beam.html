How to play

  • A grid of mirrors (/ and \) flashes for a moment — memorise where they are.
  • The mirrors hide and a laser fires from one edge. Before the timer runs out, tap the border where you think the beam comes out.
  • The beam replays to reveal the truth: a hit scores (faster & combos = more), a miss or timeout costs a life. Grids grow and mirrors multiply. P pause · R restart · M mute.
  • Online: 2–4 players, same puzzle. Answers stay hidden until the replay reveals who hit — if some hit and some miss, the ones who missed lose a life. Last one standing wins.
