How to play

  • Mirrors ( and ) appear briefly — memorise where they are.
  • They vanish, a laser fires in; tap the edge where it exits before time runs out.
  • A replay reveals who hit — when some hit and some miss, those who missed lose a life. Last one standing wins.
  • Solo faces 3 CPU rivals; Online is 2–4 real players. A long match keeps speeding up. R restart · M mute.
