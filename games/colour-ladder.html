How to play

  • Coloured balls fall down the lanes. Land each in the basket of its own colour; a wrong basket costs a life.
  • Press and hold a rung, then drop it elsewhere — each rung steps a ball across to the next lane, so you redirect the falls. On Hard, a rung lets its own colour pass straight through.
  • You have a few movable rungs. After you move one it recharges briefly before it can move again — so you plan across all of them.
  • Faint grey bars are fixed walls that block every colour and can't be moved — route around them.
  • A ball marked + is a faster heal ball — land it in its colour to regain a life; missing it is harmless.
  • Over time it gets busier and a little faster. P pause · R restart · M mute.
