How to play

Look at the color for 4 seconds, then recreate it within 25 seconds. Lock it in when ready; your current answer is submitted when time runs out.

Drag a wheel up or down. Use ± or arrow keys for small adjustments; hold Shift for finer control. Page Up/Down changes by 16; Home/End jumps to a limit.

Closer colors score higher. Speed does not affect the score. Each answer starts from the same neutral gray.

To improve game balance, we may retain match questions, answers, and results without account IDs or player names for internal analysis.
