How to play

To improve game balance, we may retain match questions, answers, and results without account IDs or player names for internal analysis.

Memorize the color for 4 seconds. You then have 25 seconds to recreate it. Lock your answer when you are ready; when time runs out, your current color is saved automatically.

Drag a wheel upward to increase a channel. Use ± or arrow keys for single steps, Page Up / Down for 16, and Home / End for the limits. Hold Shift while dragging or scrolling for finer control.

Your score measures color similarity. Taking more or less time does not change it. Each round starts from the same neutral gray.
