Throwing controls

  • Press in the board or mode panel, flick upward, and release to throw.
  • Direction and speed just before release determine the landing point. Flick sideways to steer; a faster upward flick usually lands higher.
  • The press point does not affect your aim. Start wherever feels comfortable.
