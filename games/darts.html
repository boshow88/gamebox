How to play

  • Press, flick toward the board, and release. You throw from below the bull, so a gentle flick up aims at the centre.
  • The release is what counts: pull sideways at the last moment and the dart drifts that way; flick a touch harder and it flies flatter and higher. The same flick always lands the same — no random spread.
  • Drag anywhere (the press point is ignored). D debug view · R reset session · M mute.
