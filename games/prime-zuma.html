How to play

  • A turret in the centre shoots prime balls; the chain spirals inward and you lose if it reaches the core.
  • A shot prime p divides every ball in a contiguous run divisible by p (length 3+) by p — any that become 1 are removed.
  • Gaps split the chain; matching ends pull together and crash — primes can chain more clears. P pause · R restart.
