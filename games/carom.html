How to play

  • Hot-seat for two players. On your turn, drag one of your players back and release to flick it — like a slingshot.
  • Carom off teammates, rivals and the ball to knock the ball into the far goal. Knock it into your own net and the point goes to your rival.
  • Right-click (or the corner ✕) cancels a drag so you can pick another player. First to 3 goals wins. R restart · M mute.
