How to play

  • Remove every piece. Each slides straight toward its white end cap.
  • Drag to rotate the puzzle. Tap a piece or its numbered button to pull it out. A blocked piece stays in place; its blockers turn amber.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom. Keyboard: arrows to rotate, 1–9 to pull, Home to reset the view, Ctrl+Z to undo.
