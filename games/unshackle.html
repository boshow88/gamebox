How to play

  • Remove every piece. Each slides straight toward its white end cap.
  • Drag to rotate the puzzle. Tap a piece or its numbered button to pull it out. A blocked piece stays in place; its blockers briefly flash amber.
  • Pinch or scroll to zoom. Keyboard: arrows to rotate, 1–9 to pull, Home to reset the view, Ctrl+Z to undo.
  • Three of one colour fuse into one star treat. The plate holds five pieces; a completing third piece goes directly to fusion.
  • Tap a waiting piece to slide it back along its original path. Make room by matching or returning pieces. Optional colour symbols help identify matching groups.
