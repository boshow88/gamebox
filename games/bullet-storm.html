How to play

  • Bullets stream in from every edge. Weave your dot through the gaps — your score is how long you survive.
  • On a mouse, the dot follows your cursor. On touch, drag anywhere — lift and re-press to re-anchor.
  • A hit costs a life; at zero lives the run ends. P pause · R restart · M mute.
