How to play

  • Merge two touching tiles with the same number: the tile you move disappears and the one it joins doubles.
  • Emptied cells stay empty and nothing slides, so plan which tiles meet. Reduce the board to a single tile to win.
  • A merge that lands on a ×2 cell doubles once more: 4 + 4 there makes 16.
  • Tap a tile, then a highlighted neighbour, or drag one onto the other. Keyboard: arrows move, Enter selects and merges, Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes.
  • Hint explains one merge you can work out. Reveal shows a full solution from the current position.
