How to play

  • Each round, drag a rune and line up 3+ of a colour to clear, chaining combos — the cleared runes damage your rival.
  • The rival raises a shield each round (clear enhanced / N+ combos / a named colour…). Meet it or your attack is blocked. Heart runes heal you; 5+ of a colour forges an enhanced rune.
  • Then the rival strikes back. Drop their HP to 0 to win. P pause · R restart · M mute.
