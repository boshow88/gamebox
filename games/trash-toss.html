How to play

  • Set an angle, hold to charge, release to throw. Power cycles 0 → 100 → 0, with each sweep taking 1.3 seconds.
  • Large bins score 10, small bins 25. Hit a moving obstacle for −5. Earlier trash stays and can block future throws.
  • Each scene has ten exclusive objects with fixed weights. All scenes use the same throwing strength. Construction has five barrels at increasing distances. Weather changes between rounds; rain increases air resistance.
  • Practice has no clock. Matches have five rounds and 12 seconds to release. Both players get identical trash and weather per round. The field and moving obstacles continue through turns.
  • Ties add up to three extra rounds, then draw. Change settings and press New game to apply. P pauses, R returns to ready, M mutes.
